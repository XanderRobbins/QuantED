OUR MISSION

WHAT IS
QUANT ED?

QuantED is the University of Florida's hub for quantitative finance and computational thinking. We bridge the gap between financial theory and practical implementation, preparing students for careers in quant trading, fintech, and data science.

Every session focuses on one of our three pillars — Code, Finance, or Algorithms — building a complete toolkit for the modern quant.
